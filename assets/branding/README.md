# OpenSAE Brand Assets

- `opensae_banner.png` — approved full horizontal OpenSAE wordmark. Use for wide headers, organization banners, and dataset introductions.
- `opensae_symbol.png` — approved standalone symbol. Use in compact navigation and square logo slots.

These are the original user-provided PNGs. Preserve their pixels, proportions, and colors. Do not redraw or replace them with other logo styles.

Their usage and deployment requirements are in [`work_plan/08_opensae_brand_identity_unification.md`](../../work_plan/08_opensae_brand_identity_unification.md).
